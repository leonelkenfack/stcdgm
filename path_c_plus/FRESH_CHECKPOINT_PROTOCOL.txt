WHY THIS PROTOCOL EXISTS
------------------------
The Round-2 smoke test produced a false-positive PASS because:
1. The smoke notebook copied V5_DIR/epoch_last.pth → SMOKE_DIR/epoch_last.pth
   ONLY IF SMOKE_DIR/epoch_last.pth did not exist (Cell 3 `if not exists`).
2. The smoke #1 run saved its (random-init) encoder + (10-epoch-from-random)
   weights back to SMOKE_DIR/epoch_last.pth at end of finetune_bundle_b.
3. Smoke #2 reloaded the polluted SMOKE_DIR ckpt instead of fresh V5_DIR.
4. Result: encoder appeared "loaded strict=True" but with smoke-#1-polluted
   weights, diffusion fell back to strict=False (UNet shape changed across
   smoke runs).

The Q_phys 0.40 → 0.40 outcome was uninformative (DS analysis: probability
0.346 under Bernoulli null, most probable value).
